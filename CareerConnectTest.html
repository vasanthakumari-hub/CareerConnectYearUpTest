About This Program

Career Connect is a 6-week program designed to help college students build job skills, confidence, and community as they prepare to launch their careers.

Apply Now

How It Works

Flexible Workshops

Join virtual, interactive workshops at times that fit your schedule.

Community Engagement

Connect with peers and program leaders throughout the sessions.

Learning Hub

Access helpful tools, resources, and ongoing support after the program.

Key Dates

  • Registration Deadline: October 7
  • Kickoff Sessions: October 5–8
  • Program Begins: Week of October 12
  • Final Enrollment Deadline: October 16

Who Can Apply

  • Enrolled with a Career Connect partner college, or
  • 18 or older and pursuing a degree/certificate at a U.S. community college
  • Available 2–4 hours per week for the 6-week program
